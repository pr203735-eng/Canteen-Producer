College canteen order counter

Staff (producers) place food orders on a limited counter. Students (consumers) pick them up. Switch synchronization off to watch race conditions corrupt the counter.

Project brief: Producer–Consumer problem

ProducerCanteen staff cook food orders and place them on a counter with a fixed number of slots.
ConsumerStudents pick up orders from the counter. Each order is served to exactly one student.
Buffer full / emptyempty semaphore makes staff wait when full. full semaphore makes students wait when empty.
Race conditionA mutex allows one person at a time to change the counter, so no order is lost or taken twice.
